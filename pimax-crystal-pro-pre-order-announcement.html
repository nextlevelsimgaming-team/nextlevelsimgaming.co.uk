What is native foveated streaming?

With eye tracking built in, the Crystal Pro knows where you are looking. Foveated rendering uses that to concentrate GPU effort on the centre of your gaze, and Pimax's foveated streaming applies the same idea to the wireless link, so the sharpest part of the image goes where your eyes are. The idea is that you get a cleaner picture without needing as much raw bandwidth or GPU power.

It's an interesting approach for sim racing, where the centre of your view carries the dash, mirrors and braking markers. I'll be testing exactly that when I get my hands on one.

The headline specs

  • 2880x2880 per eye, around 17 million pixels in total
  • QLED with Mini-LED local dimming, 576 zones per eye
  • 110 degree horizontal field of view and 35 pixels per degree
  • 72, 90 and 120Hz refresh rates
  • Integrated Omnivision eye tracking
  • Wi-Fi streaming included, optional third-generation 60G AirLink, or wired DisplayPort 1.4a
  • SLAM 2.0 inside-out tracking, with optional Lighthouse support and an optional MR faceplate
