Privacy & Cookies

Short version: nothing is tracked on this site until you say yes, and you can change your mind at any time.

Last updated 14 September 2026

Who this is

nextlevelsimgaming.co.uk is run by Gary, the person behind the Next Level Sim Gaming YouTube channel. There's no company or data-processing team behind this — it's one person's site.

Analytics — Google Analytics

This site can use Google Analytics (GA4) to see which guides and videos are actually useful — page views, roughly how long people stay, and which country traffic comes from. IP addresses are anonymised before Google stores anything.

Analytics is off by default on every visit. It only switches on if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner, which sets an analytics cookie from Google. If you click "Decline", or don't choose at all, no analytics cookie is set and nothing is sent to Google.

Affiliate link tracking

Some outbound links to partners (Pimax, Tobii, StudioForm, HONS VR, AMVR, Pizzter) record an anonymous event — which partner, which page, which button — so I can tell which guides actually drive value for the partners backing the channel. This only fires through the same Google Analytics setup above, so it's also off unless you've accepted analytics cookies. No personal or order information is captured; that happens on the retailer's own site, under their own privacy policy.

What's stored either way

Whatever you choose, one thing is saved in your browser's local storage (not a cookie): a flag remembering that you made a choice, so you're not asked again on every page. It never leaves your browser and isn't read by this site's server.

Changing your mind

Every page has a "Cookie settings" link in the footer that reopens the banner so you can switch between Accept and Decline whenever you like.
